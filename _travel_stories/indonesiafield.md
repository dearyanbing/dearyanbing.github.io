What intoxicated me the most in Bali was not its surfing, massaging or beach, but all the moments that I sat in a motorcycle back seat venturing across the fields.

It's precisely when the wind blows half-way till the mountains that one could hear the whisper of simple happiness resonating with their heartbeat.
